Key Insights from the Iris Dataset

1. Species Distribution
The Iris dataset contains three species of flowers: Iris-setosa, 
Iris-versicolor, and Iris-virginica. The species count chart helps 
us understand the distribution of each species in the dataset.

2. Sepal Length Comparison
The box plot shows the distribution of sepal length across the three Iris
species. It helps compare their median sepal lengths, variation, and 
possible outliers.

3. Relationship Between Petal Length and Petal Width
The scatter plot shows the relationship between petal length and petal 
width. Flowers with longer petals generally tend to have wider petals, 
indicating a positive relationship between these two measurements.

4. Differences Between Iris Species
The scatter plot helps distinguish the three species based on petal 
measurements. Iris-setosa generally forms a separate group, while 
Iris-versicolor and Iris-virginica show some overlap.

5. Petal Measurements Help Identify Species
Petal length and petal width are useful features for distinguishing Iris 
species. Iris-setosa generally has smaller petals, whereas Iris-virginica
generally has larger petals.

6. Statistical Summary of the Dataset
The statistical summary provides information about the mean, standard 
deviation, minimum, maximum, and quartiles of the numerical columns.
These statistics help us understand the typical values and variation in 
flower measurements.
